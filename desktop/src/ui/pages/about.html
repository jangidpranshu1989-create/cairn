Cairn

Cairn is an agentic browser built in India. It compares prices across stores, reads articles aloud, turns pages into summaries, PDFs and mind maps, and asks your phone before any payment.

AI by Groq and NVIDIA NIM. Built on Chromium via Electron. Ad & tracker blocking by Ghostery's open-source engine. Typeface: Inter by Rasmus Andersson (SIL OFL). Icons: Lucide (ISC).
