3.4.1: Automatic updates. Cairn now downloads new versions in the background, checks them and installs them when you're not using the app (no taps on Android 12+ after the first update; turn it off in Settings → About). Saved cards and addresses are only offered on the main page, never inside ads or frames. Cairn Cloud is more stable and secure: no more crashes on interrupted AI answers, safer live connection and sign-in.
