<p><b>Just say it. Cairn gets it done.</b></p>
<p>Cairn is a fast Android browser with an AI agent built in. Ask in Hindi, Hinglish or English. Cairn browses, compares prices across trusted stores, fills your cart and stops at the UPI PIN screen so you stay in control.</p>
<p><b>What's new in 3.0</b></p>
<ul>
<li><b>New home:</b> a personal greeting, one big composer with a Search / Agent switch and voice, your delivery address, clean shortcuts and "Continue your journey" cards.</li>
<li><b>Exact-match shopping:</b> brand, model, storage and colour must match. Covers, chargers, keypad phones and stores with a trust score below 70 are removed.</li>
<li><b>Checkout Magic:</b> Find → Cart → Address → UPI. Cairn fills your saved address, selects your UPI ID and pays only within the limit you set. You always enter the UPI PIN yourself.</li>
<li><b>Rich answers:</b> comparison tables with a highlighted best pick, spec cards, code blocks with one-tap Copy, callouts, numbered citations and source cards.</li>
<li><b>Circle to Ask:</b> long-press the Cairn stone and circle anything on a page to ask about it.</li>
<li><b>Faster tabs:</b> WebView pre-warming and shimmer loaders everywhere.</li>
</ul>
<p><b>Also included</b></p>
<ul>
<li>Shield blocks ads and trackers; private tabs; Safe Browsing.</li>
<li>Read-aloud for articles, mind maps, PDF saving, price-drop alerts.</li>
<li>Passwords and PINs are masked from the AI. Fingerprint approvals for payments.</li>
<li>Sync with Cairn on Windows, macOS and Linux.</li>
</ul>
<p>Cairn is free, and its source code is public on GitHub. AI features use the Cairn Cloud service (cairn-cloud.onrender.com) or your own Groq/NVIDIA key.</p>
