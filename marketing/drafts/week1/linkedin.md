# LinkedIn founder post — Day 2 (Mon 23 Nov, 9 am IST). Link in first comment.

Kota se ek solo developer ke roop me, maine pichle kuch mahine ek cheez pe lagaye: Cairn, India ke liye free AI browser.

Idea simple tha. Hum log "sabse sasta", "15k ke andar", "Hindi me samjhao" jaise search karte hain. Lekin zyadatar AI tools English-first hain, aur agent wale features paid hain.

Cairn me aap bolte ho, aur wo khud browse karta hai: trusted stores pe price compare, exact model filter, cart tak. Payment se pehle fingerprint, aur UPI PIN hamesha aap khud daalte ho.

Sabse bada sabak: AI ko paise ke paas le jaana aasaan hai, usse safe banana mushkil. Isliye amount AI se nahi, page se padha jaata hai.

Abhi users bahut kam hain, aur main pehle 1,000 tak pahunchna chahta hoon. Aap try karke honest feedback denge?

Aap ke hisaab se Indian users ke liye AI shopping me sabse bada darr kya hai?
