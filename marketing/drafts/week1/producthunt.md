## Maker comment
Hi Product Hunt, I'm Pranshu, a solo developer from Kota, India. I built Cairn because shopping and research on Indian sites means 10 tabs, fake listings and English-only AI tools. With Cairn you just say "₹15k ke andar sabse sasta 5G phone", and it browses, filters to the exact model and shows a comparison with sources.

The design rule I cared about most: the agent can fill your cart, but by default it can't pay without your fingerprint, and the amount comes from the page, not the AI.

It's free and Apache-2.0. I'm here all day. What should it learn next?
