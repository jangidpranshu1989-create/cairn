Cairn for Developers
OAuth 2.0 · OpenID Connect · PKCE

Add "Continue with Cairn" to your app

Cairn is a standard OpenID Connect provider. Users signed in to the Cairn browser on Android or desktop get a one-tap sign-in sheet; everyone else sees the Cairn consent page.

1. Register your app

Sign in to your Cairn Account to register apps.

Sign in

Your apps

2. Drop in the button

Or use the declarative form. A cairn:signin event fires on document with { user, tokens, idToken }.

3. Verify on your server

Send the id_token to your backend and verify it with any OIDC library using the discovery document. Check iss, aud (your client ID), exp and nonce.
