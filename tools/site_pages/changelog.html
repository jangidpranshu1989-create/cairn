What's new.

Release notes for Cairn on desktop and Android.

v3.0.3 Nov 2026

  • AI that keeps working when Cairn Cloud can't be reached: after your own keys, the phone and desktop now go straight to three keyless backups in turn: Kilo Gateway (free NVIDIA Nemotron), OVHcloud AI Endpoints (gpt-oss-120b, Qwen) and Pollinations. Each one has its own limits, so one being busy no longer stops you.
  • LLM7 removed: since November 2026 it needs a token and answered every anonymous request with "429".
  • Cairn Cloud: Groq → NVIDIA NIM → Gemini → OpenRouter → Kilo → OVHcloud → Pollinations.
  • Image questions (lasso, "what's in this picture") also fall back to free vision models.
  • When Cairn adds something to your cart without the AI, the reply now says whether the cart confirmed it.
  • Clearer error details: a 404 from the hosting edge is labelled as such.

v3.0.2 Nov 2026

  • Fixed for good: "Cairn's AI is busy right now (Cairn Cloud 404 · LLM7 429)". Cairn now wakes the cloud server as soon as the app opens and, if it is still starting, waits for it patiently (about 45 seconds, with a "Waking up Cairn Cloud…" status) instead of giving up after 2 seconds.
  • Shopping works even with no AI at all: "iphone 17 flipkart pe dhundh ke cart me daal do" now finds the product, compares the stores, adds the pick from the store you named to the cart and opens checkout, all on the device.
  • Cairn also tries its built-in server address if a saved one stops answering.
  • Redesigned home-screen shortcut icons (New tab, Private tab, Ask Cairn) with clearer labels.
  • The dock now shows the full word "Assistant" on every phone size.

v3.0.1 Nov 2026

  • Fixed: "Cairn's AI couldn't be reached" in chat and shopping. The server now retries every AI provider and never passes a retired provider's error to the app.
  • Add to cart in one go: after comparing prices Cairn adds the best pick (or the store you named) to the cart straight away, even when the AI is busy.
  • Flipkart, Croma and other stores get 18 seconds on slow mobile networks; Amazon pages load with a standard Chrome for Android identity.
  • Image and file uploads work on every site (Product Hunt, X, Gmail): multiple files, extension filters and the camera.
  • Safer payments: autopay uses the total shown on the checkout page; if it is missing or differs from what the AI read, Cairn asks for your fingerprint.
  • Desktop: payment buttons are also recognised by the checkout URL, card/UPI fields and Hindi labels.
  • Safe Browsing is on in Android tabs and insecure (http) content is blocked on https pages.
  • New favicon, Apple touch icon and web app manifest for the website.

v3.0.0 Nov 2026

  • New bento home: live greeting, time and weather pills, sync badge, glass Omni-bar with voice, camera OCR and agent mode.
  • Clean shortcuts grid (HD favicons, no duplicates or blank pages) and "Continue your journey" cards.
  • Shimmer skeletons for answers, history, downloads and remote tabs; paged history with infinite scroll.
  • Faster tabs with WebView pre-warming.
  • Exact product matching: brand, model, storage and colour must match; covers, keypad phones and low-trust listings are removed.
  • Autonomous Find, Cart, Address and UPI flow within your limit; you always enter the UPI PIN.
  • Rich answers: comparison tables, spec cards, code blocks with Copy, callouts, numbered citations and source cards.
  • Default server is now cairn-cloud.onrender.com on Android, Desktop and the website.
v2.7.0
20 November 2026Sharper agent, permanent cloud

Fixes from real-phone testing of 2.6.0, a quicker home screen, and a Cairn Cloud database that survives restarts.

Agent taps the right button

  • On-screen reading (OCR and vision) now captures only the web page, so the chat window and dock can't be read or tapped by mistake. Before, the agent could tap the word "Add" inside its own chat message.
  • Add to Cart, Buy Now and Go to Cart are found as real buttons in the page, including Flipkart's and Amazon's fixed bottom bars. If the button isn't on screen, Cairn scrolls once and looks again.
  • On-screen matching only taps the whole button label, such as "add to cart". It never taps a single word in a sentence.

Fewer rate limits

  • The agent sends about 60–70% less page data per step: only labelled buttons, links and fields, plus visible prices. Older page views are dropped from the conversation.
  • When Groq says 429, Cairn waits 1.5 seconds and retries on llama-3.1-8b-instant. If that is limited too, it rests that key and moves straight to the next one.
  • Settings, AI now takes several free keys at once (Groq, Gemini and OpenRouter) and rotates between them.

Home and speed

  • New home screen: one glowing box for searching, opening sites or giving Cairn a task, one-tap task chips, and a card to continue your last chat.
  • Android keeps the visible tab's page process at high priority, so it is less likely to be closed under memory pressure.

Cairn Cloud server

  • Optional permanent storage on Supabase's free tier. When anything changes, the server saves a compressed copy of its database within 15 seconds, plus a final copy on shutdown and one dated backup per day. It restores everything on boot, including login keys, so free hosts like Koyeb no longer lose accounts.
v2.6.0
20 November 2026Agent that finishes the job

The assistant no longer stops at product cards. It compares, adds the best deal to your cart, goes to checkout and can pay within a limit you set.

No more "Not found"

  • Fixed the bare "Not found" under shopping results. Cairn's old free AI endpoint was retired upstream. AI requests now move automatically to the next working provider on 404, 429, timeouts or outages.
  • If every provider is down, you still get an on-device recommendation from the compared prices, along with the exact error codes.
  • Optional: add your own free Groq, Gemini or OpenRouter key in Settings, AI, as an extra backup.

Autonomous shopping

  • Say "add the cheapest one to cart and take me to checkout". Cairn compares stores, opens the best trustworthy listing, closes popups, enters your delivery PIN, picks the variant, taps Add to Cart, checks that it was added, then opens checkout.
  • Self-healing taps: when a site's button can't be found in the page code, Cairn reads the screen with on-device OCR, then vision, and taps the button directly.
  • Live reasoning feed: see what the agent found and what it will do next, for example "Lowest trusted price: ₹20,999 on Flipkart, adding to cart".

Agent payments (UPI)

  • New in Settings, AI: save your UPI ID, then set a per-payment limit and a daily limit. Turning this on requires your fingerprint.
  • For payments within your limits, the agent approves the payment itself, selects UPI, enters your ID and taps Pay. You finish the payment with your UPI PIN in your UPI app, as NPCI rules require. Anything above your limit still asks you first.

Fixes

  • The live mini-browser no longer covers chat messages. Inside the assistant it now appears in the chat, and outside the assistant you can drag it, but not off screen.
v2.5.0
20 November 2026New dock, fewer crashes

A redesigned two-row dock, a calmer home screen and a much sturdier browser, based on testing 2.4.1 on real phones.

Dock

  • New two-row dark dock: a rounded address pill (menu, current site, reload) above Back, Forward, the Assistant pill with a voice button, Tabs and New tab.
  • Tap Assistant to chat, tap the waveform to talk. The dock still hides while you scroll down and returns when you scroll up, without covering the page.

Home and Guard

  • The "Cairn Guard Active" pill no longer sits on top of Jump back in. It now appears only while a password, PIN or OTP field is focused on a web page.
  • Blank pages, error pages and chrome-error addresses are no longer saved as shortcuts or history, and duplicate sites are merged.

Stability

  • Crash safety net: errors are logged on the device and Cairn recovers instead of closing. If a restart is needed your tabs come back.
  • When a web page's renderer crashes only that tab reloads; pages that keep crashing stop retrying and show an error.
  • Background network and AI tasks can no longer take the app down.

Sign-up

  • The sign-up captcha is now drawn as a proper image that always fits, instead of getting cut off at the top.
v2.4.1
20 November 2026Chats and camera

Cairn's assistant now remembers your conversations, reads book pages from the camera, and the browser scrolls like it should.

Assistant

  • Chat history: every conversation is saved on your phone, grouped by Today, Yesterday and earlier. Search, pin, rename, delete, and reopen any chat to continue it. Private-tab chats are never saved.
  • Short AI-written chat titles, a live aurora orb, shimmering status text and a glowing composer while Cairn works.
  • Camera photos are full resolution and auto-rotated. Text in book pages is read on your device (English and Hindi/Devanagari), so "explain this chapter" works even without a vision model.
  • Circle to Ask now reads the text inside the circle on-device and the selection matches what you drew.
  • Shared answers end with "Found via Cairn AI Browser".

Browser

  • Scrolling back up on YouTube and other app-like sites scrolls the page instead of refreshing it. Pull to refresh can be switched off in Settings → General.
  • The bottom dock no longer resizes pages while it hides and shows, which fixes jumping and broken layouts on some sites.
  • The new tab page greets you by name with the real local time, and has a profile button for your account, chats and settings.
  • The website and all links moved to our own address; nothing points to cairn.app any more.

Cloud

  • Optional Turso database so free hosts keep your account after restarts. One-click Render and Koyeb deploy guides.
  • Vision fallbacks: Groq Llama 4 Scout, Gemini 2.5 Flash, NVIDIA.
  • Android releases are now signed.
v2.1.0
19 November 2026Answers with sources

Cairn now has its own search, answers with live sources, and an account you can create in a second.

Search

  • Type a site's name — "youtube", "amazon kholo", "open flipkart" — and Cairn opens the site directly. No search page in between.
  • Cairn Search: Cairn's own results page with a streamed AI answer, numbered citations that link to each source, related searches and follow-ups. No third-party branding.
  • Two independent indexes in parallel, so results still load when one is slow or blocked on your network.

Assistant

  • Every factual answer is grounded in a live web search and cites its sources inline, with an "N sources" list you can open.
  • Attach photos, camera shots and files (PDF, Word, PowerPoint, Excel, text and code). Text is read on your device; scanned pages and images are understood by the vision model.
  • Copy with sources, share, listen, retry and suggested follow-up questions on every answer.

Account and home screen

  • Create a Cairn account instantly — no codes, no captcha and no "check your internet connection". It's stored encrypted on your phone (PBKDF2, 210,000 rounds).
  • Two home-screen widgets on Android: a search bar, and quick actions for New tab, Private, Ask and Photo.
v2.0.0
19 November 2026Cairn 2.0

A new look and a much more capable agent. Cairn now ships with AI built in — install it and start asking.

Agent

  • Live store-by-store price comparison with skeleton loaders, image fallbacks and trust scores.
  • Checkout Magic: your encrypted delivery profile fills name, email, phone and address after sign-in, then stops at the payment page for your approval.
  • Coupon and bank-offer finder matched to the cards you save (only bank names and last four digits).
  • Swarm: up to three sub-agents work in parallel — for example flights, hotels and cabs for one trip — and combine the result.
  • Mention any open tab with @ to use it as context, and circle anything on screen to explain, translate, copy or find it cheaper.
  • Price-drop and back-in-stock alerts, checked in the background.
  • Strict language mirroring: Cairn answers in the language you write in.
  • Cairn Guard: a visible badge while the agent works on sign-in and payment pages; passwords, PINs and card fields are masked from the model.

Browser

  • Live tab thumbnails, high-resolution site icons, a floating glass dock with spring animations and system haptics.
  • Picture-in-picture preview of the agent's background tabs, and Recall — private, on-device full-text search across pages you've read.
  • Stay signed in to shops between sessions; Google services removed from defaults (Have I Been Pwned checks).
  • New stacked-stone logo, Inter typeface and charcoal palette with a sandstone accent across Android, desktop and the website.

v1.0.0
19 November 2026First release

The first public release of Cairn — an agentic browser that understands Hindi, Hinglish and global languages.

Agent

  • Multi-step browsing agent that searches, opens pages, clicks, types, selects and scrolls in real tabs, with a live step log and stop button.
  • Groq (GPT-OSS 120B / 20B) and NVIDIA NIM (Nemotron) with automatic fallback between providers.
  • Voice input with Whisper large-v3 turbo.
  • “Ask before” policy for purchases and sign-ins, or every action.

Shopping

  • Parallel price comparison across Amazon.in, Flipkart, Croma, Reliance Digital, Vijay Sales and Tata CLiQ, plus US and UK stores.
  • Variant matching, accessory filtering and 0–100 trust scores.
  • Add to cart and checkout up to the payment step, with phone approval.

Reading & page tools

  • Background read-aloud of any article with mini-player and speed control.
  • AI summary, save as PDF, interactive mind map with PNG export, long screenshot, reader mode.
  • Send files to your phone into a folder you choose.

Browser

  • Password manager with OS-keychain encryption, generator and CSV export; passkey sign-in to Cairn accounts.
  • Cairn Shield tracker blocking, per-site permission prompts, Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control.
  • History, bookmarks, downloads, find in page, zoom, dark/light themes.

Android companion

  • QR pairing, biometric payment approvals with screenshot protection, and file receiving into Download/Cairn.
