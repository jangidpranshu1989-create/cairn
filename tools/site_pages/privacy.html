Summary

  • No ads, no data sales, no tracking pixels. Cairn has no advertising business and does not sell or rent personal data.
  • Anonymous usage stats. The Android and desktop apps send a random install id created on your device (not linked to your account, phone number or hardware ids), the event name (for example app opened, still in use, search made, agent started), Cairn version, OS version, language and time zone. We never receive the sites you visit, page titles, what you search or type. Turn it off any time in Settings → Privacy → Share anonymous usage stats. Counts are kept as daily totals.
  • Browsing data is local. History, bookmarks, downloads, saved passwords and settings are stored on your computer.
  • AI goes through Cairn Cloud. When you use the assistant, the relevant text is sent through Cairn Cloud to an AI provider (Groq, NVIDIA, Google Gemini or OpenRouter, then the free Kilo Gateway, OVHcloud AI Endpoints or Pollinations if those are busy). Cairn does not store prompts or answers.
  • An account is optional. You only need one for phone pairing, payment approvals, file transfer and sync.
  • The agent cannot pay on its own. Payments always require your explicit approval.

Data stored on your device

The desktop browser stores the following locally in your user profile folder. We cannot see it.

Websites you visit receive the same information they would from any Chromium browser. Cairn Shield blocks known ad and tracker domains, and Cairn can send Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control signals (on by default).

AI requests

When you ask the assistant something, Cairn sends the information needed for that task to the AI provider. Depending on the task this can include your message, the text of the current page or a page the agent opened, a list of the page's buttons, links and form fields, a selection you made, or a voice recording for transcription.

  • Cairn Cloud AI (all apps): the desktop and Android apps send AI requests to our server, which forwards them to Groq, NVIDIA, Google Gemini or OpenRouter. If those are unavailable, requests fall back to free public gateways (Kilo Gateway, OVHcloud AI Endpoints, Pollinations), which may apply their own logging and terms. We do not store prompts or responses; we only keep a daily request counter to enforce fair-use limits. Apps no longer store or use personal AI API keys.
  • Voice: when you tap the mic, the audio clip is sent once to the speech-to-text provider (Groq Whisper, through Cairn Cloud) to get the text of what you said. The recording is not stored by Cairn, and the mic is only on while the mic button is active.
  • Password fields are never sent to AI. Values typed into password, card number, CVV and PIN fields are masked before the page is described to the AI.

Cairn Cloud account

If you create an account, our server stores:

  • Your name and email address, and your password as a bcrypt hash (we never store or see the plain password).
  • Passkey public keys and credential IDs (the private key never leaves your device).
  • Active sessions, with device name, user agent and IP address, so you can see and revoke them.
  • Paired phones (device name and platform).
  • Payment approval requests: a title, the merchant and amount the agent showed you, and your decision. Requests expire after 3 minutes.
  • Files you send to your phone, held until the phone downloads them or for 24 hours at most (max 100 MB each).
  • Sync data, only if you turn on sync (rolling out). Passwords are encrypted on your device before upload, so we cannot read them.
  • A security log of sign-ins, password changes and account deletion.

We use this data only to provide the service — signing you in, delivering approvals and files, and preventing abuse. We send email only for account security (verification and password reset) unless you join a waitlist.

Cairn for Android

Cairn for Android stores its sign-in token in the Android Keystore and keeps a connection to Cairn Cloud so approvals arrive instantly. Biometric checks are performed by Android; we never receive fingerprints or face data. Files you accept are saved to Download/Cairn or the folder you named. The app has no third-party analytics or advertising SDKs. It sends anonymous usage counts to Cairn Cloud (see “Anonymous usage stats” below), which you can turn off in Settings → Privacy.

This website

The Cairn website uses no cookies and no third-party scripts. It counts visits itself: a random visitor id kept in your browser's local storage, the page path, the referring site's domain, campaign tags in the link, mobile or desktop, and your time zone. Nothing is counted if your browser sends Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control. Fonts are self-hosted. If you use the contact form or the Pro waitlist, we store your name (optional), email, message and IP address (to block spam) and use them only to reply to you or tell you about Pro.

Who we share data with

  • AI providers (Groq, NVIDIA, Google Gemini, OpenRouter, and the fallback gateways Kilo, OVHcloud and Pollinations) — only the content of requests you make, as described above.
  • Hosting and email providers that run Cairn Cloud on our behalf, under contracts that limit use to providing the service.
  • Authorities, only when required by valid law and after reviewing the request.

Online stores the agent visits for you see a normal browser visit. When you ask the agent to buy something, the checkout details you approved are submitted to that store, under that store's privacy policy.

Retention

Account data is kept while your account exists. Transfers are deleted after download or 24 hours; approval requests after 30 days; contact messages after 12 months; security logs after 12 months. When you delete your account, your account record, sessions, passkeys, devices, transfers and sync data are deleted immediately; backups roll off within 30 days.

Your rights and choices

  • Access and export: export your passwords as CSV from Passwords; request a copy of your account data by email.
  • Delete: delete your account from the Account page in the browser (cairn://account), or email us.
  • Correct: email us to correct your name or email address.
  • Withdraw consent: sign out, unpair your phone, or remove your API keys at any time.

These rights apply to everyone, including under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the GDPR. You can also contact our Grievance Officer at GitHub issues; we respond within 30 days.

Security

Connections use TLS. Passwords are hashed with bcrypt, sessions use short-lived tokens with rotating refresh tokens, sign-in is rate-limited and CAPTCHA-protected, and accounts lock after repeated failures. See our Security page for details and how to report a vulnerability.

Children

Cairn is not directed to children under 13, and a parent or guardian must consent for users under 18 where required by law. Purchases made with the agent are subject to the store's own age rules.

Changes and contact

If we materially change this policy we'll update the date above and notify account holders by email before the change takes effect. Questions: GitHub issues or our contact page.
