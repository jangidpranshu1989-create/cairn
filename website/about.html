About

The web should work for you, in your language.

Cairn means charioteer — the one who drives so you can focus on where you're going. We're building a browser that does the clicking, comparing and form-filling, while you stay in charge.

Why we built it

Most people don't talk to computers in perfect English.

They say “show me the cheapest iPhone” or “read this article to me” — often in two languages at once. AI browsers so far have been built for a narrow audience. Cairn is built in India, for Hindi, Hinglish and every language people actually mix — and for the real tasks of daily life: buying the right thing at the right price, reading, learning and sharing with the phone in your hand.

What we believe

  • Agents should be visible. You see every step and can stop it.
  • Money needs a human. Nothing is paid without your approval.
  • Privacy by default. Your browsing stays on your device. No ads, ever.
  • Fast beats flashy. We run on Groq and NVIDIA NIM so answers arrive in seconds.
  • Honest rankings. No affiliate deals, no paid placement.
