Features

A full browser, with an agent that actually does things.

Everything you expect from a modern browser — tabs, passwords, privacy — plus an assistant that can browse, click, type, compare and save on your behalf.

The agent

Tell it the goal. Watch it work.

Saarthi plans multi-step tasks and carries them out in real tabs: searching, opening pages, reading, clicking buttons, filling forms and scrolling. Every step is shown live in the assistant panel, and you can stop it at any time.

  • Any language in, your language out — Hindi, Hinglish, English, Bengali, Marathi, Tamil and more.
  • Voice input with Groq Whisper; tap the mic and speak.
  • Asks before it acts on anything sensitive — choose “purchases only” or “every action” in Settings.
  • Hands back to you for logins, OTPs and CAPTCHAs, then continues.
  • Context aware — ask about the page you're on, or select text and ask.

Checkout to approval

Adds to cart, confirms the delivery address with you and stops at payment for your biometric approval.

Reading

Listen to the web.

Ask Saarthi to read any article — even one you haven't opened. It fetches the page in the background, cleans it up and reads it aloud in a natural voice, while you keep browsing.

  • Mini-player with pause, speed (0.8× to 2×) and stop.
  • Hindi auto-detected from Devanagari text.
  • Reader mode for distraction-free reading.

Android companion

Approvals and files, on the phone in your pocket.

The Saarthi app pairs with your browser by QR code. It shows a full-screen approval with amount and merchant, unlocked only by your fingerprint, face or PIN, and saves files the browser sends you.

  • Works on Android 8.0+, keeps a light background connection with automatic reconnect.
  • Tokens stored in the Android Keystore; approval screens block screenshots.
