One sentence, start to finish

From “I want this” to the payment page — without lifting a finger.

01 · Ask

Say it the way you'd say it to a friend.

Find me the cheapest iPhone 16 128GB from a trusted store

Type or speak — Cairn understands Hindi, Hinglish, English and dozens more, and replies in your language. Voice input runs on Groq Whisper.

02 · Compare

It checks every major store in parallel.

Amazon.in, Flipkart, Croma, Reliance Digital, Vijay Sales and Tata CLiQ load side by side in hidden tabs. Accessories, refurbished units and the wrong variant are filtered out, and every listing gets a trust score from seller, rating, review count and discount sanity.

03 · Cart & checkout

“Add it to my cart” — and it's done.

Cairn opens the product, adds it to your cart and walks through checkout using the address saved in your store account — clicking, typing and scrolling in a real browser tab you can watch. Logins, OTPs and CAPTCHAs are handed back to you.

04 · Approve on your phone

Payment waits for your fingerprint.

At the payment step the agent stops. Your paired Android phone shows the merchant, item and exact amount; approve with fingerprint, face or PIN — or deny. Nothing is ever paid without you.

Questions

Good to know.

Is Cairn free?
Yes. The browser and Android app are free. AI is built in, so everything works the moment you install it. Cairn Pro with hosted AI is coming soon — see pricing.
Can Cairn pay without me?
No. The agent always stops at the payment step. With a paired phone you approve using biometrics; without one, you confirm in the browser and enter your UPI PIN, card OTP or wallet authentication yourself, just like normal.
Which stores does price comparison cover?
In India: Amazon.in, Flipkart, Croma, Reliance Digital, Vijay Sales and Tata CLiQ. In the US and UK it uses major local retailers. Some stores occasionally block automated visits — Cairn tells you when that happens and still compares the rest.
Does it work in Hindi and other Indian languages?
Yes. Ask in Hindi, Hinglish, Bengali, Marathi, Tamil, Telugu or any language the models understand. Replies match your language unless you pick one in Settings. Read-aloud uses the voices installed on your computer.
What data leaves my computer?
Only what's needed to answer you: your request and the relevant page text go to the AI provider you chose. Browsing history, passwords and bookmarks stay on your device. Read the privacy policy.
Is there an iPhone app?
Not yet. Cairn for Android is available today; iOS is on the roadmap.
