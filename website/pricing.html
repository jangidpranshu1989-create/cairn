Questions

Pricing, plainly.

Do I need an API key?
No. Cairn ships with AI built in. If you prefer, you can add your own Groq or NVIDIA key in Settings → AI; Cairn encrypts it with your device's keychain and sends it only to that provider.
How much does the AI cost me?
For typical personal use, nothing — both providers' free tiers are enough for dozens of tasks a day. If you exceed them you pay the provider directly at their published rates.
Does Cairn take a commission on purchases?
No. We don't use affiliate links and we don't rank stores for money. The cheapest trustworthy result wins.
When does Pro launch?
We'll email the waitlist first. Free stays free — Pro is for people who don't want to manage a key.
