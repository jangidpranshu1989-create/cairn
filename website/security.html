Security

An agent you can trust with your cart.

Cairn is designed so that the most powerful thing it does — acting on websites for you — never turns into acting without you.

Human approval for payments

The agent stops at every payment step. Approval needs your phone's biometrics, or an explicit click in the browser if no phone is paired. Requests expire after 3 minutes.

Encrypted on your device

Saved passwords and API keys are encrypted with your operating system keychain. Nothing is readable from the profile folder alone.

Passkeys and strong sign-in

WebAuthn passkeys, bcrypt password hashing, CAPTCHA after failed attempts, account lockout and rate limiting.

Sensitive fields masked

Password, card number, CVV and PIN values are masked before any page is described to the AI model.

How it's built

Security details.

AreaProtection
Browser sandboxWebsites run in sandboxed Chromium renderers with context isolation; browser pages use a strict IPC allowlist
Agent permissionsConfigurable “ask before” policy: purchases only, or every action; you can stop any task instantly
Logins & CAPTCHAsDetected and handed back to you; the agent never types your passwords or solves CAPTCHAs for you
SessionsShort-lived access tokens with rotating refresh tokens; see and revoke sessions from your account
Phone pairingOne-time QR codes that expire in 5 minutes; per-device tokens stored in the Android Keystore
Approval screenFull-screen, screenshot-blocked, unlocked only by Android biometrics or device credential
File transferPer-account, authenticated download; deleted after delivery or 24 hours
Transport & serverTLS everywhere, security headers (Helmet), strict CORS, input validation and request size limits
Tracker blockingCairn Shield blocks known ad, tracker and cryptominer domains
Responsible disclosure

Found a vulnerability? Tell us.

Email GitHub issues with steps to reproduce. We acknowledge reports within 2 working days, keep you updated, and credit you on our changelog if you'd like.

Please

  • Give us reasonable time to fix the issue before disclosing it publicly.
  • Only test against your own accounts and data.
  • Avoid privacy violations, data destruction and service disruption.

In scope

  • Cairn for desktop and Cairn for Android
  • the Cairn website and Cairn Cloud

We won't take legal action against good-faith research that follows these guidelines.
